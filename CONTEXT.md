# Jet & Nicole Wedding Site

The wedding site for Jet Ysalina and Nicole Ormita. The site speaks as the couple ("us"), even where an account belongs to only one of them.

## Language

**Save the date**:
The early announcement of the wedding date, before invitations go out.
_Avoid_: Teaser, announcement

**Invitation**:
The later announcement that asks guests to reply; it supersedes the **Save the date**.

**RSVP gate**:
The password check guests pass before seeing the RSVP details.
_Avoid_: Login

**Gift**:
Money sent to the couple by anyone who wants to celebrate them, whether or not they are attending. Money gifts are preferred over physical gifts, but are never required.
_Avoid_: Registry, payment, donation

**Gift option**:
A way to send a **Gift**: currently Venmo or Zelle. Each option names the account the guest will see on the receiving end.
_Avoid_: Payment method

## Relationships

- An **Invitation** implies the **Save the date**; a guest with an **Invitation** never needs the **Save the date** separately
- A **Gift** is sent through exactly one **Gift option**
- **Gift options** are not behind the **RSVP gate**
- The RSVP page is only reachable from an **Invitation**; a **Save the date** never shows it
- The wedding date and location are shown only with a **Save the date** or an **Invitation**, never to a visitor who arrives without either

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Gift money to me" — resolved: gifts go to the couple ("us"), even though the receiving accounts are Jet's.
